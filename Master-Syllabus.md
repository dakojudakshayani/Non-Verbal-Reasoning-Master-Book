## Standard Learning Method
Definition → Purpose → Visual Basics → Rules and Patterns → Step-by-Step Logic → Solved Examples → Different Approaches → Practice → Placement Questions → Competitive Questions → Common Mistakes → Revision → Mastery Test

## Learning Levels
- Level 1: Beginner – Basic shapes, figures and visual observation.
- Level 2: Foundation – Classification, series and analogy.
- Level 3: Intermediate – Mirror images, paper folding and embedded figures.
- Level 4: Advanced – Cubes, dice, matrices and spatial visualization.
- Level 5: Exam Preparation – Placement and competitive examination questions.
- Level 6: Mastery – Complex problems, timed practice and mock tests.

## Repository Goal
Build a complete beginner-to-advanced Non-Verbal Reasoning resource that develops visual observation, pattern recognition, spatial awareness and figure-based problem-solving skills.
